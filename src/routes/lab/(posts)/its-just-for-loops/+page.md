Freya Holmér's [2021 tweet](https://x.com/FreyaHolmer/status/1436696408506212353) expanded my understanding of maths in a small but genuinely important way. $\Sigma$ and $\Pi$ — those symbols that look like they belong to a completely different discipline — are just for loops with a fancier hat.

I never would have made that connection on my own. Predictably, the replies descended into pedantry — mathematicians insisting the analogy was imprecise, missing the point entirely. Freya was trying to make something feel less intimidating, and it worked.
